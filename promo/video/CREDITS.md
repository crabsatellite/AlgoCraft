## Sound effects

Kenney (https://kenney.nl):

- [Interface Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds)
- [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio)

All three packs are [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original notices are in `licenses/`. Selected files were converted to stereo 48 kHz WAV and renamed; `scripts/prepare_promo_assets.py` records their source names. Additional `whoosh`, `riser`, `sub` and `shimmer` effects are generated by that script.

## Fonts

Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono and Press Start 2P are bundled through their Fontsource npm packages under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copies of the package license notices are retained in `licenses/`. The dependency lockfile pins the font packages used to render.

## Models, textures and captures

AlgoCraft computer/trophy model geometry, UI and problem assets come from this repository. Vanilla item/block textures and genuine Minecraft screenshots come from Minecraft 1.21.1, Mojang/Microsoft; the CC0 sound license does not apply to them. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang/Microsoft.

The production Web IDE and Judge were recorded through an isolated JVM demonstration fixture. Native world/IDE images come from the sealed hidden-client acceptance captures. Motion-graphic calendars, server topology, loot cards and engraving metadata are illustrative. They should not be presented as extra in-game menus.
